\# Write Lesson



\## Preparation



1\. Read AGENTS.md and MASTER\_PROMPT.md.

2\. Read CURRICULUM.md.

3\. Read PROGRESS.md.

4\. Identify the requested lesson.

5\. Read prerequisite lessons if they exist.

6\. Check the glossary for existing terminology.



\## Research



Before writing:



1\. Identify fundamental engineering concepts.

2\. Research official documentation.

3\. Examine real-world implementations.

4\. Compare existing approaches.

5\. Identify trade-offs and failure modes.

6\. Gather references.



Do not invent citations or claim to have verified

sources that you could not access.



\## Writing



Follow the lesson structure from MASTER\_PROMPT.md.



Write for experienced software engineers.



Prioritize:

\- Deep technical understanding

\- Internal mechanisms

\- Architecture

\- Production engineering

\- Hands-on examples

\- Verification strategies



Avoid marketing language and generic AI advice.



\## Quality Review



Before finalizing:



1\. Check factual accuracy.

2\. Check completeness.

3\. Review code and commands.

4\. Validate citations.

5\. Check consistency with earlier lessons.

6\. Check practical exercise acceptance criteria.



\## Deliverables



Create or update:



lessons/<section>/<lesson-id>/lesson.md

lessons/<section>/<lesson-id>/references.md

lessons/<section>/<lesson-id>/exercises.md



Update PROGRESS.md.



Report unresolved issues and unverified claims.
